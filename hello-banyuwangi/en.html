Hello Banyuwangi · English for Tour Guides

Privacy Policy

How the Hello Banyuwangi app handles information while you learn practical English for tourism.

Developer: ZamoPiVersion: 1.0.0Last updated: November 19, 2026
In short: Hello Banyuwangi is designed for offline learning. It does not require an account or send your bookmarks or learning activity to ZamoPi.

1. About the app

Hello Banyuwangi (Android package ID: com.zamopi.hellobanyuwangi) is an English-learning app for tour guides. It provides modules, vocabulary, useful expressions, local context, conversations, and audio that can be used without an internet connection.

2. Data collection

For the version covered by this policy, ZamoPi does not require user accounts and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts through the app.

The app does not include advertising, user analytics, or tracking services that transmit usage activity to the developer.

3. On-device information

If you use the bookmark/favorites feature, your selections are stored locally on your device through app preferences. This information is used to display your saved learning content.

ZamoPi does not receive these bookmarks from the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling it usually removes locally stored data. Android system backup and restore, if enabled, may operate according to your device settings and platform policies.

4. App permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, or location permissions. Its Android package may contain the ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission to check connectivity status. This permission does not itself allow transmission of personal information.

Learning images and audio are bundled as local assets so educational content is designed to work offline.

5. Third parties and distribution

The app includes software components for audio playback and local preferences. The reviewed version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs used to collect user data.

If you obtain the app through Google Play, Google may independently process installation, download, security or store usage data under its own policies. Opening this privacy page in a browser may result in GitHub Pages processing technical access data under its own policies.

6. Security and deletion

Bookmarks are stored on your device and are protected by your device's local storage and security mechanisms. You can clear local app data in Android settings or uninstall the app, subject to system backups that may exist.

Hello Banyuwangi has no ZamoPi user account to delete from a server.

7. Children's privacy

The app provides educational content and does not ask for users' personal identities. If data collection practices or target audiences change, this policy and applicable child privacy obligations will be reviewed.

8. Policy changes

ZamoPi may update this policy when app functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The current text and update date will be maintained on this page.
